# Technique Guide (all skills)

> **Preliminary, not reviewed by calisthenics experts.**
> Based on Larissa's German technique guide (*Calisthenics Technikleitfaden*, 7 levels, 51 entries), which draws on the references listed at the end. This file is the base for the camera rules in `src/exercises.js`. Only the push-up rules are tested; all other skills are **beta** and need testing with real people (and ideally a coach) before the feedback can be trusted.

## General principles

### Body line
- **Natural curve:** For most skills the lower back may keep a slight curve. A *growing* arch together with ribs moving forward or a dropping pelvis matters more than a single frame.
- **Long levers** (push-up, dragon flag, front lever, planche): judge the connection of trunk, pelvis and legs.
- **Wanted rounding:** Crow, frog stand and tuck variations are compact shapes. A wide, slightly rounded upper back is part of the position. L-sit, V-sit and manna come from active hip compression. In the pistol squat the trunk may lean forward.
- **Overhead and support:** In a straight handstand the shoulders push up actively. In the L-sit the hands press down. On the bar the shoulder blades move during a pull, so the right cue depends on the phase.

### Breathing
Keep breathing in holds. In reps, breathe in during the easier phase and out during the hard part. If breath and form are lost together, shorten the hold or the range.

### Live feedback rules for the app
- Stable camera, support surface or bar visible.
- **Side view** for body line and depth. **Front or angled view** for symmetry, rotation and one-arm positions.
- **The camera can judge:** body angles, direction of movement, visible asymmetry.
- **The camera cannot judge:** muscle tension, hand pressure, lower back contact. Use hints or a self-check instead.
- If joints are hidden, say "Please adjust the camera" instead of guessing.
- Give one short cue only when an error is clearly visible over several frames.
- Handstands and levers need a practiced exit. Peak loads on rings, the hefesto and one-arm transitions need proper equipment, step-by-step assistance and a qualified coach. Stop on sharp pain or loss of control.

## Camera views
| Key | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| `side` | Side on to the camera |
| `angled-side` | Side, turned slightly toward the camera |
| `angled-front` | Front, turned slightly to the side |
| `front` | Facing the camera (or the body surface facing the camera) |
| `side+front` | Needs both views (line from the side, symmetry from the front) |

## Skills

Columns: **Camera check** = what pose detection could measure. **Self-check** = what the user must feel or confirm, because the camera cannot see it. **Cue** = the main live feedback sentence.

### Level 1 – Basics
| Skill | View | Camera check | Self-check | Cue |
|-------|------|--------------|------------|-----|
| Classic push-up | side | Shoulder–hip–ankle line, elbow depth, chest and hips move together, head in line | Core, glutes, thighs lightly tensed | "Lower your chest and hips together." |
| Inverted row | side | Hip in line with the trunk, same body angle at start and end of each rep, chest reaches the bar | Ribs down, pressure through the heels | "Keep your hips level with your body." |
| Hollow body hold | side (low camera) | Legs and shoulders lifted, leg angle, hold time | Lower back stays on the floor | "Raise your legs a little." |
| Crow pose / frog stand (bent arms) | side or angled-front | Feet off the floor, hips above the base, elbows stay bent, hold time | Hands press into the floor | "Lift one foot first." |

### Level 2 – First steps
| Skill | View | Camera check | Self-check | Cue |
|-------|------|--------------|------------|-----|
| Elbow lever with foot assist | side | Hip height relative to the trunk, toes still touching | Elbows stay close to the belly | "Shift a little more weight onto your hands." |
| Hanging knee raise | side | Knee height, swing before the rep, pelvis curls at the top | Grip and steady breathing | "Lift your knees without swinging." |
| Tuck L-sit | side | Arms straight, feet off the floor, hips not sinking between the hands | Shoulders pushed down, away from the ears | "Push yourself higher out of the support." |
| Pike push-up | side | Hips stay high (inverted V), head path forward and down, depth | Ribs stay close to the pelvis | "Keep your hips up while your head lowers." |
| Wall handstand | side | Shoulders and hips above the hands, banana shape (ribs forward) | Push tall through the shoulders | "Push tall and bring your ribs over your pelvis." |
| Frog stand (straight arms) | angled-side | Elbows straight, both feet off the floor, hold time | Wide upper back, steady hand pressure | "Arms long, shift your weight forward slowly." |
| Pull-up | angled-side | Chin above the bar, arms fully straight at the bottom, leg swing | Controlled shoulders at the start | "Pull with your elbows and keep your legs still." |
| Bar dip | side or angled-front | Elbow depth, shoulders not falling forward, leg swing | Shoulders stay active over the bars | "Lower slowly, keep your legs still." |

### Level 3 – First free skills
| Skill | View | Camera check | Self-check | Cue |
|-------|------|--------------|------------|-----|
| Tuck dragon flag | side | Knee angle stays constant, hip moves with the knees | No pressure in the neck | "Keep your knees tucked and move your hips with them." |
| Tuck back lever | side | Trunk roughly horizontal, knees close to the trunk, arms straight | No pulling pain in shoulder or elbow | "Keep your knees tight and your body level." |
| Free elbow lever | side | Shoulder–hip–ankle roughly horizontal, feet off the floor, hold time | Elbows do not slide | "Stretch your legs back and bring your hips to chest height." |
| L-sit | side | Legs roughly horizontal, knees straight, arms straight, trunk not leaning back | Hands press the bars down | "Arms long, straighten your knees a little more." |
| Archer push-up | angled-front | Chest moves toward the working hand, pelvis parallel to the floor | More pressure on the working side | "Chest to your working hand, hips parallel to the floor." |
| Free handstand | side | Hands, shoulders, hips and feet stacked, banana shape | Push tall through the shoulders | "Push tall and bring your feet over your hips." |
| Tuck front lever | side | Trunk roughly horizontal, chest up, knees close to the chest, arms straight | Shoulders stable under load | "Arms long, lift your hips a little higher." |
| Pistol squat | angled-front | Heel stays down, knee follows the toes, free leg stays forward, depth | Whole foot under load | "Heel down, knee toward your toes." |

### Level 4 – Advanced
| Skill | View | Camera check | Self-check | Cue |
|-------|------|--------------|------------|-----|
| One-leg dragon flag | side (+ from the feet) | Trunk and long leg in one line, no hip kink | Pelvis does not rotate open | "Keep your body and long leg in one line." |
| Tuck human flag | front | Trunk sideways and roughly horizontal, knees tucked, arms long | Bottom arm pushes, top arm pulls | "Push away with the bottom arm and lift your hips." |
| Straight dragon flag negative | side | Body stays straight while lowering, stop when the hip leaves the line | No pressure in the neck | "Stop lowering before your hips give in." |
| Tuck planche | side | Feet off the floor, knees free of the arms, hips near shoulder height, arms straight | Shoulder blades pushed apart | "Keep your knees free and push the floor away." |
| Advanced tuck front lever | side | Hip open, thighs roughly across the trunk, trunk horizontal | Ribs stay down | "Bring your knees a bit closer and keep your hips up." |
| Wall handstand push-up | side | Ribs over the pelvis, depth, arms fully straight at the top | Head never carries weight | "Keep your ribs over your pelvis and lower slowly." |
| Full dragon flag | side | Trunk, hips and legs move as one straight lever, no kink, no swing | No pressure in the neck | "Lift your hips and feet together." |
| Bar muscle-up | angled-front | Pull height, both elbows over the bar together, leg kick | Grip | "Pull higher first, then both elbows over the bar together." |

### Level 5 – Very advanced
| Skill | View | Camera check | Self-check | Cue |
|-------|------|--------------|------------|-----|
| One-arm elbow lever | angled-side | Trunk and legs roughly horizontal, pelvis rotation | Wrist stable under load | "Turn your pelvis back a little and stretch your legs back." |
| V-sit | side | Legs above horizontal, knees straight, arms straight | Hands press the bars down | "Press the bars down and keep your knees long." |
| Advanced tuck planche | side | Hip open, pelvis near shoulder height, trunk horizontal, arms straight | Shoulder blades pushed forward | "Knees in a little and push the floor further away." |
| Straddle front lever | side+front | Hip in line with the shoulders, knees straight, both legs at the same height | Arms stay long | "Straighten your knees, hips to shoulder height." |
| Full back lever | side | Body horizontal and straight, no sagging pelvis | No pulling pain in shoulder or elbow | "Lift your waist and reach your legs back." |
| Strict ring muscle-up | angled-front | Both sides move together, rings stay close, no leg kick | False grip held | "Rings close to your chest, both shoulders over the rings together." |
| Free handstand push-up | side | Legs stay over the base, depth, arms fully straight at the top | Never rest on the head | "Lower slowly and keep your legs above you." |
| One-arm push-up | angled-front | Both sides of the pelvis level, little rotation, depth | Legs press into the floor | "Keep both sides of your pelvis level." |

### Level 6 – Expert
| Skill | View | Camera check | Self-check | Cue |
|-------|------|--------------|------------|-----|
| Straddle planche | side (+ from behind) | Shoulders to hips horizontal, legs symmetric, arms straight | Shoulder blades pushed apart | "Hips in line with your trunk, arms long." |
| Full human flag | front | Body sideways and horizontal, legs together | Bottom arm pushes, top arm pulls | "Legs together, hips up, push further away with the bottom arm." |
| Full front lever | side | Body horizontal, hips in line, knees straight | Ribs stay down | "Lift your hips and straighten your knees." |
| Press to handstand | side | Hips over the hands before the legs rise, no jump, arms straight | Shoulders keep pushing | "Hips over your hands, then lift your legs." |
| One-arm handstand | side+front | Hips over the supporting hand, supporting shoulder pushed up | Free hand ready to return | "Push the supporting shoulder up and keep your hips over your hand." |
| One-arm pull-up | angled-front | Chin reaches the bar, arm fully straight at the bottom, no kick | No pulling pain in elbow or shoulder | "Pull your elbow down to your side and keep your legs still." |
| Hefesto | side | Smooth pull without a jerk, hips stay close to the bar | Coach present, no pain at the biceps tendon | "Pull without a jerk and keep your hips close to the bar." |

### Level 7 – Elite
| Skill | View | Camera check | Self-check | Cue |
|-------|------|--------------|------------|-----|
| Full planche | side | Shoulder, hip and ankle horizontal, legs together, arms straight | Shoulder blades pushed apart | "Body level, arms long, push the floor away." |
| Iron cross | front | Body vertical, both arms horizontal and at the same height, arms straight | Coach and assistance required | "Keep both arms at the same height." |
| Full planche push-up | side | Chest and pelvis move together, feet stay up, arms straight at the top | – | "Move your chest and hips together, straighten your arms at the top." |
| One-arm front lever | side+angled-front | Pelvis at shoulder height, working arm straight, rotation | Grip without sudden changes | "Keep your pelvis at shoulder height and your working arm long." |
| One-arm muscle-up | angled-front | Smooth transition, rotation | Coach present, load judged on site | "Move through the transition calmly." |
| Manna | side | Legs closed and straight, roughly horizontal, pelvis rises, arms straight | Strong hip compression | "Knees long, lift your pelvis with control." |
| Maltese | side+front | Body horizontal at ring height, hips in line, rings at the same height | Coach and assistance required | "Hips in line and both rings at the same height." |
| Victorian | side+front | Body horizontal on the back, hips up, legs together, rings steady | Coach and assistance required | "Lift your hips, legs together, rings steady." |

## Safety levels for the app
- **Coach required** (iron cross, maltese, victorian, hefesto, one-arm muscle-up): the app should never present these as solo training. Show a clear coach-and-equipment warning.
- **Practiced exit required** (handstands, levers, back lever): show the exit tip before the set.
