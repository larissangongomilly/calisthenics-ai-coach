# Push-up Form Specification

Reference for the rules the coach checks. Each rule lists the camera view that can measure it.

## Starting position (high plank)
- Hands shoulder-width or slightly wider, palms flat, fingers forward.
- Shoulders roughly above the wrists, arms straight.
- Head in line with the spine, eyes on the floor.
- Core, glutes and thighs lightly tensed.
- Shoulders, hips and heels form one line (side view).

## Lowering
- Both elbows bend at the same time, moving back and out at about 45° to the torso.
- Chest leads toward the floor. Chest and hips lower together.
- Chin slightly tucked, neck long. Shoulder blades move gently together.
- Go as low as stability allows, ideally chest close to the floor.
- Breathe in. When practicing, lower over 2 to 3 seconds.

## Pushing up
- Press the floor away and breathe out.
- Chest and hips rise together.
- Finish with straight arms, shoulder blades spreading slightly.

## Common errors and camera view
| Error | View |
|-------|------|
| Hips sag or stay high | Side |
| Chin leads (head forward) | Side |
| Chest rises before hips | Side |
| Not deep enough | Side |
| Elbows flare out | Front |
| Shoulders shrug toward ears | Front |
| Left and right side uneven | Front |

## Coach behavior
- Start with the side view.
- If errors point to something the side view cannot see, the coach asks the user to turn to face the camera for the next set.
